Methods, data and interpretation

Data source and method labels are part of the result, not optional metadata.

Historical reanalysis

Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, ERA5/ERA5-Land. Reanalysis combines observations with a numerical weather model; it is not a direct station record. For long-term trends, choose a consistent reanalysis product.

Climate projections

Open-Meteo Climate API serves downscaled CMIP6-related climate-model data, bias-corrected to ERA5-Land. The available regional high-resolution model set covers 1950–2050; its future period and scenarios must be interpreted within the provider's documentation.

Trend and extremes

OLS slope is descriptive and may be sensitive to autocorrelation, missingness and nonlinearity. Heat days are calculated from daily maximum temperature. Baselines use available overlap; missing baseline years are disclosed.

Scientific guardrail: do not interpret reanalysis as station observations, and do not treat the climate API's model hindcast as observed past climate. For publication, preserve the source/version, inspect missing values, use consistent baseline periods, evaluate serial correlation and compare multiple models. This static dashboard does not claim to provide an official forecast.
